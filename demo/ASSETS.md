# Demonstration images: sources and licences

Seven images of real places in and near Jabalpur, used **only** on the demonstration site and only
as images of those places. All were vetted in [design 02](../docs/design/02-palette-validation.md),
and their licences were re-checked against the Wikimedia Commons API on 2026-10-13. They are the
960 px thumbnails Wikimedia Commons generates, used unmodified. None contains GPS metadata (checked)
or identifiable private individuals (checked visually). An eighth candidate ("Gwarighat") was
**excluded** because it shows identifiable people bathing.
